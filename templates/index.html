🔍 Case Studies & Real Edge Scenarios

1. Extreme Heat Forces Day 0 Harvest

When temperature exceeds 38°C and humidity is high, the baseline price-seeker waits for ₹38/kg instead of ₹30/kg. However, due to 65% spoilage, waiting produces near zero revenue, whereas immediate harvest protects ₹21,000+ in pocket earnings.

2. Cold Storage Unlocks Massive Future Price Gain

With cold storage preserving spoilage below 4%, the farmer can safely delay harvest to Day 10, capturing a 68% surge in future price (yielding +₹30,000 additional profit over Day 0).

3. Missing Weather / Price Data Recovery

When weather APIs or prices are unavailable in low-bandwidth rural areas, the system automatically uses regional historical fallbacks and warns the user without crashing.

🛡️ Responsible AI, Limitations & Ethical Disclosure

Decision Support Only

This simulator is a decision-support helper based on statistical decay equations and market costs. It does NOT replace farmer visual inspection or physical crop assessment.

Transparency & Uncertainty

Future prices and weather forecasts carry natural uncertainty. Results are labeled as synthetic simulation estimations and must be validated under local mandi conditions.

Data Privacy & 100% Offline

Zero farmer telemetry is uploaded to remote servers. The simulator functions completely in the browser cache, respecting low-bandwidth village constraints.
